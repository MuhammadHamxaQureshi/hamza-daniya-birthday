Hamari kahani

Kuch chapters jo waqt ke saath meri zindagi ka hissa ban gaye.

Pehli mulaqat

Hum first year mein coaching academy mein mile. Tumhe pehli nazar mein dekh kar mere dil mein ek ajeeb si khushi aur kashish paida hui. Main tumhe chup chup kar dekhta aur dheere dheere tumhari taraf khinchta chala gaya.

Academy ki woh seat

Main roz sab se pehle academy aane laga. College ki chutti hoti to seedha academy aata aur tumhare peeche wali seat pakar leta. Jab tum mere samne aa kar baithti, main chupke se tumhe dekhta. Tumhe dekh kar mujhe bohat sukoon milta tha.

Register mein ek number

Ek din Physics ki class mein light chali gayi. Guard ke generator start karne ke un 1–2 minutes mein maine tumhare register mein apna number rakh diya aur dheere se tumhare kaan mein kaha: “Maine number rakh diya hai.”

Mere paas simple Nokia phone tha—WhatsApp bhi nahi tha, is liye tumhare WhatsApp messages ka mujhe pata hi nahi chala. Phir ek din ek unknown number se SMS aya: “Tum wohi ho?” Mera dil bohat tez dharkne laga. Yahin se hamari pehli baat shuru hui.

Raat bhar ki baatein

Maine dost se phone udhaar liya aur ghar mein sab se chup kar tumse WhatsApp par baat karna shuru ki. Hum roz poori poori raat baatein karte, ek doosre ka khayal rakhte aur dheere dheere bohat attached ho gaye.

Jab tumne kaha, “I love you too,” to meri aankhon se aansu nikal aaye. Woh meri zindagi ke sab se emotional aur khubsurat moments mein se ek tha.

Pehla imtehan

Abu ko phone ka pata chala to unhon ne phone le kar tor diya. Mujhe phone se zyada dukh is baat ka tha ke tumse baat kaise hogi. Kuch din baad maine phir doston se phone udhaar liya aur tumse rabta kiya, kyun ke hamara bond mere liye bohat important tha.

Families ko batana

Tumne apni bari behan Faryal ko hamare baare mein bataya. Unhon ne mujhse baat ki aur mujhe samjha. Phir tumne apni ami ko bataya aur main unse mila—unko bhi main acha laga. Yeh hamare liye umeed ka waqt tha.

Maine bhi apni ami ko bataya. Abu ami ne pehle narazgi dikhayi aur contact band karne ko kaha, lekin main unko razi karne ki koshish karta raha. Main ghar ki har baat maan kar unka trust jeetna chahta tha.

Intezaar aur umeed

Do saal guzarte gaye. Tum mere liye aane wale rishton ko mana karti rahi aur mera intezar karti rahi. Abu kehte rahe ke pehle kisi field mein jao, university mein admission lo, phir baat karenge. Main bhi tumhare liye apne parents ko yaqeen dilane ki koshish karta raha.

Doori ka chapter

University mein Software Engineering ka admission hua aur main Abbottabad aa gaya. Tum Kohat mein thi aur hum door ho gaye. Main parhai par focus kar raha tha taake family ko yaqeen ho ke main apna future bana raha hoon, lekin distance ne hum dono ko bohat udaas kiya.

Tumhari khala apne bete ke liye tumhara rishta maang rahi thi, jab ke tum mera intezar kar rahi thi. Is waqt mein hum dono ne ek doosre ka bohat saath diya.

22 May 2026 — teen din

Eid ki chuttiyon mein tumhari ami ki call aayi. Unhon ne kaha ke agar teen din mein rishta bhej sakte ho to theek, warna woh tumhara rishta kahin aur kar denge. Woh waqt bohat mushkil tha. Main rota raha aur unse request karta raha ke hamare pyaar ko ek mauqa dein.

Phir Eid ke pehle din tumhara message aya: “Mere liye rishta bhejo, warna sab kuch khatam.” Maine himmat kar ke apne bare bhai ko bataya. Unhon ne Abu se baat karne ka wada kiya.

Ek umeed, phir khamoshi

Abu ne kaha ke woh tumhari family ke baare mein maloomat karenge. Mujhe ek umeed mili aur maine Allah se dua ki. Kuch waqt baad Abu ne kaha ke unhein family theek nahi lagi, is liye rishta nahi karenge. Yeh baat mere liye bohat painful thi.

Maine tumhe sab bataya, lekin tumhe laga ke maine tumhe dhoka diya. Main chahta hoon ke tum jaano: meri taraf se koshish aur feelings sincere thin, lekin halaat mere control mein nahi thay.

28 May 2026 — aakhri baat
